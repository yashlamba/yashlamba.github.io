Learning latte art

Learning latte art with a small story
November 16, 2026 · 1 min · Yash Lamba

I got access to a fancy coffee machine and immediately wanted to do some fancy latte art. The first few attempts were blobs, actually even getting a blob wasn’t easy.

Then, on a random weekend, I visited a cafe and asked for cappuccino. It wasn’t busy, so I asked the nice barista lady if she can tell me the correct way to steam the milk. She was very kind and taught me everything step by step. I took my coffee and was about to leave when she offered me chance to make a coffee. I hopped on the opportunity and went behind the counter (so happy and excited!). She guided me through all the steps and I got to pour a coffee for the next customer - who was offered a free coffee, fair enough.

Unfortunately, it wasn’t my star moment and it turned out ot be a blob, but I at least knew what I did wrong. Also, one of the best experiences as well!

Since then I have made some decent latte art. I will use this page as my gallery until I lose interest.
